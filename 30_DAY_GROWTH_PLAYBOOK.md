## 2. Organic Growth Accelerators (How to Hit 2,000 Followers Fast)

Facebook pages with 0 followers don't get organic distribution automatically until they feed the algorithm initial engagement.
- Identify top Gujranwala Facebook Groups:
  1. *Voice of Gujranwala*
  2. *Gujranwala Updates / News*
  3. *Gujranwala Pehlwan & Foodies*

### B. High-Converting Visual Cards
Text-only posts get virtually zero reach on Facebook. Our automated PIL engine generates branded 1080x1080 high-contrast cards with:
- Deep forest green & gold accents (national aesthetic).
- Bold readable headlines for mobile devices.
- Verified Source badge (`📌 Dawn`, `📌 Express News`, `📌 Geo`).
- "Follow Hamara Gujranwala" CTA directly on the image.

### C. The Hero & Pride Multiplier
People in Gujranwala take enormous pride in their wrestling history (Gama Pehlwan, Inam Butt) and local food delicacies.
- Tributes to local legends generate 5x more shares than regular posts.
- Every share exposes the page to 200–500 new friends of the sharer for free!

## 3. Strict Ethical, Legal & Authenticity Guardrails

1. **Mandatory Source Verification:** Every news post must cite a recognized, authentic source (Dawn, Tribune, Geo, Dunya, BBC Urdu, APP, or District Administration).
2. **Zero Sensationalism / No Fake News:** Never publish unverified rumors or hearsay.
3. **Strict Cybercrime & Safety Compliance:** Strictly avoid defamatory content, violence, explicit material, sectarian issues, or illegal acts.
4. **Community Respect:** Tone is always polite, respectful, and dignified.
